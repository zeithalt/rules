## 2. Move Order

`🏰 Factions Game` `⚔️ Global Map`

> *Cycle* confusingly can refer to two things:
> - generally in `🏰 Factions Game` - one full round of all factions' turns.
> - during a `⚔️ Global Map` battle - each one hour interval when the score is measured.

- The move order is determined by a [#random roll](<https://discord.com/channels/562910943848169472/1263055030601388034>) on our discord server at the beginning of each cycle.

- Faction members are free to decide where to attack by voting or any other process agreed in the faction chat;

- The battle can be started by any [Key faction member](<https://zeithalt.github.io/rules/#rules_07_key_members>) using **/gm_start_attack** command.

- A faction can only **attack neighbouring territories** (where land connection exists) + **one over territory by sea.**

- A faction has **2 days to confirm or skip their turn** according to the [Battle Schedule](<https://zeithalt.github.io/rules/#rule_03_battle_schedule>).

- Same territory can not be attacked twice in a row (does not apply to [Corruption](<https://zeithalt.github.io/rules/#rules_09_corruption>) attacking the land).
