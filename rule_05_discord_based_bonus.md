## 5. Discord-based bonuses

`🏰 Factions Game` `⚔️ Global Map`

These are discord `🏰 Factions Game` features that allow factions to increase their base IP bonus in battles.

These bonuses have a big effect on the `⚔️ Global Map`:
- these are shown as "discord-based bonuses" in the `⚔️ Global Map` interface (long-press the "Attack" button to see them);
- they are effective during the whole battle and do not change;
- for example, 30% bonus means all IP spending by that team has a 30% boost in this battle.

Only attacker and defender will get bonuses from these actions, other teams will have to do their surprise interventions in battles without them.

There are two types of bonuses:

> `🏰 Factions Game` [Actions](rule_06_faction_actions_battle.html) - a set of in-lore events that factions can take to _influence_ the game.

> `🏰 Factions Game` [Map units](rule_07_map_units.html) - various units that can be moved around the map and give bonuses to faction in battle.
